prumia is a household finance app built for two real users (and a portfolio): Brazilian reais, Brazilian rules, in
production on a home server. The interface and the user guides are in Brazilian Portuguese; this page explains what it
does and how it is built.

## No bill forgotten

Each month shows what is due, what is already paid (it stays in the list, struck through) and what is overdue. Paying is
one tap. Installments and fixed bills repeat on their own, and a PDF, photo or screenshot of a payment slip (boleto)
becomes a bill through local OCR.

<Image src={mes} alt="Month view in the demo: an overdue bill highlighted, the total paid and a ruler with the due dates of the month." />

## Know whether money will be left, before it happens

The **projected balance** adds what is expected to come in and subtracts what was paid and what is still due. Each wallet
has its own: what is left on the meal voucher does not pay the rent. The emergency fund shows how many months you could
hold on.

<Image src={resumo} alt="Monthly summary in the demo: projected balance of the General wallet and one card per wallet." />

## Salary on the right day

Salary is forecast month by month with Brazilian payroll deductions (INSS and income tax), landing on the day it really
arrives: the 5th business day or the last business day, accounting for weekends and national holidays.

<Image src={receitas} alt="Monthly income in the demo: what was received, what is still expected, and per person." />

## Under the hood

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Backend" icon="seti:go">
    Go, Gin and GORM as a modular monolith (handler, service, repository), PostgreSQL, goose SQL migrations, 300+ tests with real Postgres through testcontainers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Spec-first API" icon="document">
    The OpenAPI contract is the source of truth: Go types and interfaces are generated from it, and so are the frontend's TypeScript types.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Frontend" icon="laptop">
    React 19, Vite, TypeScript and Tailwind 4, with a themeable design system (nine themes) and Vitest tests on the main flows.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Correctness by design" icon="approve-check">
    Money as int64 cents, never floats; calendar dates without time zones; per-household isolation enforced in every query and by composite foreign keys.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Delivery" icon="rocket">
    GitHub Actions CI on every pull request, Docker Compose deployment behind nginx and a Cloudflare Tunnel, backup and restore scripts.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
